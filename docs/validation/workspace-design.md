## Fresh review and fixes

Two parallel reviewers reviewed `bca201d...0a323e6` against repository standards and the approved specification. Standards reported two findings; Spec reported two findings, one overlapping.

| Finding | Resolution and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| New action could bypass a dirty note or mutate assignment after an aborted action save | Flush note, initiative, and action edits successfully before changing the pane or assigning fields. Regression first failed (`review-red-action.txt`), then passed; covers both note and action save failures. |
